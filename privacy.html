Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Nudge is a personal reminders app made by Samuel Esebor. This policy explains what Nudge stores, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: sam.esebor@gmail.com

1. What Nudge stores

  • Your account: email address and a sign-in session (Supabase Auth, or Google sign-in if you use it).
  • Your reminders: the sentence you typed or said, the title, notes, times, repeat rules, urgency, and whether it rings.
  • Places you create for location reminders: only the name you give them (for example “My room”). Nudge does not track or store your GPS location. Arrivals are reported by your iPhone’s own Shortcuts automation, if you choose to set one up.
  • Settings: time zone, preferences, and your Bark device key if you add one.
  • Google Calendar (only if you connect it): tokens that let Nudge add your reminders to your calendar.

2. How it is used

  • To show your reminders, alert you on time, and sync them between your devices.
  • Reminder sentences are sent to an AI service (Google Gemini or Groq, whichever you pick in Settings) only to turn the sentence into a reminder. Voice notes are sent to Google Gemini only to turn speech into text; the audio is not stored.
  • Urgent alerts are delivered through Bark (day.app) using the device key you provide.
  • Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

3. Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar, Nudge uses that access only to create, update and delete the events for your own reminders. Nudge’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI models, and not read by people.

You can disconnect Google Calendar in Nudge’s Settings at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Where data lives and how it is protected

Data is stored in Supabase (a hosted Postgres database). Every table uses row-level security, so an account can only reach its own data. Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone Keychain. AI keys, Google tokens and server secrets stay on the server.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Deleted reminders are removed for good after 30 days. You can delete your whole account and all of its data from Settings › “Delete my account and data”; it is erased immediately.

6. Children

Nudge is not meant for children under 13.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
