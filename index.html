Nudge

Reminders you can just say.

Type or say a sentence like “Call me tomorrow at 7am to take my meds, every weekday” and Nudge turns it into a reminder: when, how often, how urgent, and whether it should ring your phone.

What it does

  • Plain sentences, typed or spoken.
  • Rings you for urgent reminders through the free Bark app, even on silent.
  • Location reminders: “When I get to my room, put my laundry in.”
  • Google Calendar sync (optional), so reminders show up in your calendar.
  • Works offline and syncs when you’re back online.

Nudge is a personal project by Samuel Esebor.
